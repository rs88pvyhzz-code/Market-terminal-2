# Market Terminal: Release 3.4 (12 Nov 2026)

## Clean reading
Explanatory notes, caveats, source boilerplate, coverage counts and the footer are removed from every tab except Data Quality.

**Removed:**
- The page footer.
- Calendar, yield-ticker and DMO notes.
- Gilt and US Treasury source text, and the running-yield/duration line.
- The ETF issuer tracking block.
- Watchlist intro and price-source notes.
- Overview pulse footnotes.
- Analyst-target caveats.
- "Requires review" warning banners.
- Methodology fold-outs.
- Explanatory tile captions that carry no figure.
- Article retrieval timestamps.

**Kept:**
- Figures, tables, tiles with data, headlines with publisher dates, chart summaries and empty-state messages.
- Research write-ups (outlook, statements, issuer evidence), where only lines without any figure are removed.

The fund size tile now hides correctly when fund assets is shown.
